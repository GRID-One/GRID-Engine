## Decisions and rulings

No human ruling was received in this session. Decisions the package made under the authority the spec
gives it, each recorded for the owner's merge review:

- **ADR-014 proposed** (engine-spec §8.1 rule 5 delegates the question to P1-01). `grid-domain` takes
  `serde` with `derive` as its only external dependency; public contract types derive `Serialize` and
  `Deserialize` there; format crates (`serde_json`) stay in `grid-pipeline` and `grid-synth`;
  `Display` and `std::error::Error` are hand-written; no feature flag. Implemented while Proposed,
  following the ADR-011/012/013 precedent; the Product/Architecture owner accepts or rejects it at the
  merge. Rejection sends the package back (mirror types would contradict engine-output-contract §1
  rule 1).
- **Fixed names.** The Rust type names of plays-contract §11 and engine-output-contract §10 are now
  the ones `grid-domain` defines (the two sections were rewritten to match the code). Where the brief
  and a contract draft differed on a name, the brief's name was used and the contract updated: the
  lock timestamp is `Lock { kind, at }` (the §2 table wrote `lock_at`; after the second review its
  cell writes `at`, work-package Amendments row 3); `RegimeFlags` has four fields
  including `observation_noise_inflation`, because engine-spec §5.5 names it and outranks the contract
  draft's three. `PlayerKey` orders by its string form with the variant as a tiebreak so `Ord` agrees
  with `Eq`; `Week` keeps a private `u8` so that `Week(0)` cannot be built (KI-A4).
- **Exit codes** (engine-output-contract §13, new): Success 0; NoNewData 10; PartialSuccess 11;
  NetworkFailure 20; Timeout 21; RateLimited 22; AuthenticationFailure 23; InvalidResponse 24;
  SchemaFailure 25; DataValidationFailure 26; DatabaseFailure 30; LearningFailure 40; NotRun and
  Running none; usage error 2 (`EXIT_USAGE`); internal error 70 (`EXIT_INTERNAL`). P1-10 completes the
  CLI spelling and arguments.
- **Fixture-timing reading.** Engine-spec §9.5's P1-01 parity column and §7.12.3 read differently on
  when the committed canonical synthetic fixture exists. The package follows §7.12.3, §8.1 and §9.2.1
  item 7: the loader and a hand-built round-trip test now; the committed
  `synthetic-world/canonical-legacy` case, the S-1 assertion over it and the fixture guard in P1-05.
  If the owner wants the canonical legacy world exported and committed in P1-01, that needs the oracle
  exporter and is a scope change for a new package or a recorded owner decision.
- **Synchronous façade.** The `EngineCommands` and `EngineQueries` trait methods are plain `fn`
  taking `&self` and returning `Result<_, EngineError>`: the CLI is a one-shot process and may not
  depend on a runtime (engine-spec §8.1 rule 4); implementors use Tokio behind the façade from P1-02
  on (§8.13). Recorded in the `grid-pipeline` crate doc.
- **Other decisions the documents did not settle**, each with its alternatives, are in the
  implementers' reports carried into the PR body: the `ContractError` payloads (each names the
  offending key, field or drive; rule-4 `off_team == def_team` and rule-5 violations map to
  `InvalidValue`); the summary-level form of the per-draw §3.9 invariants on a record without draws;
  `to_v0_columns` returning `Result` (a `Gsis` or `Nfl` key has no v0 encoding; no invented sentinel,
  KI-P1); `LegacyV0World` gaining a `provenance` block for the producer's seeds and hash;
  `ResponseVersions` fields as `Option` with the reason for `None` per field; `Page<T>` and
  `Versioned<T>` as the two response shapes; three distinct estimate kinds (`filtered`, `predictive`,
  `smoothed_retrospective`) in `get_player_ratings`, no smoothed field in any projection record; the
  event payloads §8.4 leaves untyped; the CLI's unfixed behaviours (no arguments prints usage and exits
  0; `-V`; a bare group such as `model` is unknown, exit 2; a closed output stream is internal, exit
  70); and the usage's delivering package for the five Phase 2 outputs. The CLI implementer first
  named them by content owner (P2-01/P2-02/P2-06/P2-07) and raised it as a decision request; the
  orchestrator ruled under the authority order that every entry is P1-10 (engine-spec §8.1 target
