## Objective

block P1-12. They were recorded in `docs/00-meta/decision-register.md`: DR-D17's "To be specified" by

### What each answer carries

- **Answers 1 and 2 (DR-D14, uncovered defenders).** A defender is *uncovered* in the fold-wise fit
  that computes the play's opponent feature δ_i when he has no defensive play among that fit's training
  plays (this includes a defender seen only on offence there), or when his coefficient carries the RAPM
  `no_data` flag. His term in δ_i is his RAPM prior mean m_p (0 today), and every substitution is
  counted and persisted. A listed id with no entry in the ratings at all stays a typed failure
  (`CreditError::UncoveredDefender`), as DR-B6 already requires. The owner recorded the substitute as
  the counted-substitute exception that `layer1-credit.md` §4.3 and KI-NEW-Z4 already name, not as a
  reversal of DR-B6. `engine-spec.md` §6.8 rule 12 gets a narrow amendment that covers δ_i only. A new
  synthetic-world requirement R-7 plants defensive call-ups and fill-ins, and no real-data Layer-1 run
  that used a substitute is promoted until a pre-registered recovery check on it passes. The check's
  rates, offsets, statistics and margins stay open. The owner re-confirms m_p if DR-C9 or DR-D12 change
  how RAPM sets m_p, λ or μ_team.
- **Answers 3 and 4 (DR-D14, training rows).** The question was raised in review against the ratified
  part. Answer 3 chose the nested design on the first card set's recommendation. The second, verified
  card set reversed that recommendation, because the nested design was worse over all held-out plays on
  the planted-truth synthetic world in every seed run (scratch), and because the real-data figure that
  favoured it is per-play R², which `layer1-credit.md` §7.3 rules out as the yardstick. Before anything
  was recorded, the agent told the owner and asked again (question 4). Answer 4 supersedes answer 3:
  the ratified training rows stand. The nested design is compared with them in the context-model
  comparison that DR-C7 already requires, on weekly-credit recovery and out-of-sample forecasting, with
  statistics and a margin pre-registered first. If the nested design wins by that margin, the agent
  raises a decision request with a Proposed ADR citing DR-D14. No Proposed ADR is written now.
- **Answer 5 (DR-D17, the builder).** The agent had recommended the season-level empirical-Bayes
  forecast. The owner chose the recency-weighted pooled mean: a normal–normal posterior over the
  track's per-season mean Layer-1′ residuals in the window, with recency weights, starting from the
  base of answer 7. Its two weights per class, the base scale κ_c and the recency weight λ_c, are
  learned by rolling-origin validation on a calibration period disjoint from evaluation. Their tuning
  criterion must include calibration, because tuning on in-season log score alone drove κ_c to its
  tightest value in the toy (scratch). The criterion stays open and is pre-registered before P1-12
  tunes anything. The answer also carries the rulings the agent listed with the question: the prior is
  fixed for the season, a correction to window-season data reaches it at the next boundary, no
  changepoint flag crosses the boundary, and RAPM ratings and offseason Layer-1 credit do not seed the
  Kalman prior.
- **Answer 6 (new entry DR-D32).** The question lies outside DR-D17's recorded open part, so it gets
  its own register entry. Every season-boundary prior is built twice from the same data version: on
  S−3..S−1 for the preseason and week-1 published forecast, and on S−2..S−1 for the state that the
  season-S filter restarts from. Two canaries prove the window: S−3 data reaches no season-S state
  after week 1's forecast through a season-boundary prior, and pre-window data reaches neither prior.
  The agent's chat summary of the first canary omitted that season S's frozen V(s) and live context
  model g′_S, both fitted on S−3..S−1 under DR-C6 and DR-D15, and week 1's δ^team still carry S−3 into
  the season's observations. Review found this before recording; the canary holds those inputs fixed.
  All three options offered shared that property, so the correction does not bear on the choice.
- **Answer 7 (DR-D17, the NCAA base).** For a player with a feeder prior, that prior is the base of
  his windowed prior in every season. Every other player's base is the population prior. The base's
  weight falls only through window evidence, under the builder of answer 5. The `engine-spec.md` §2.5
  eligibility list no longer gates this prior, §18 item 3 is reworded to match, and DR-D9's thresholds
  no longer govern any Kalman prior.

- **Answer 9 (DR-D17, details found in review).** The fresh-context review of the applied recording
  found that the agent had recorded three details as decided that the owner had seen only in summary:
  the window evidence records (dV under season S's frozen V(s); the context model and team effect
  cross-fitted on the build's window with DR-D14's fold design; a team ridge with one offence and one
  defence intercept per (team, season) and no market rows), the per-season observation variance
  v_s = r_c/N_s + ω_c·Σ_w(n_w/N_s)² with r_c and ω_c estimated on the window, and the population prior
  N(m, σ²_c) per (position class, role), estimated on the window with its covariates open (DR-C9).
  Review also corrected the agent's first form of v_s, which used ω_c/G_s. The agent asked before
  anything was committed; answer 9 confirms all three as part of DR-D17.

- **Answer 10 (DR-D19, found in review).** The same review found that a stored production track
  cannot supply the DR-D19 targets that each DR-D32 build needs from its own seasons: a window
  season's production filter restarted from a prior built at an earlier boundary, around the player's
  own college prior, and used that season's own V(s), g′ and δ^team. So for any feeder-linked track
  neither canary could pass. A second read-only workflow (two research lenses, a drafter, two
  adversarial verifiers and a finalizer) drafted the question. Answer 10 sets the target, in each
  build, to the track's mean prior-builder residual in its first window season with role events, with
  its known variance, fitted by a measurement-error (Fay–Herriot-type) regression on `feeder_sv`. No
  filter run and no prior enter the target. This changes DR-D19's estimand from end-of-season smoothed
  talent to the season level, so it reverses DR-D19's 2026-10-09 decision in part. Register rule 6
  requires a reversing ADR: ADR-013 is written as Proposed and binds once the owner accepts it; until
  then DR-D19's 2026-10-09 text stands. The chosen option's description, as offered, said so: "This
  changes DR-D19's estimand from talent to season level, so it's recorded through a Proposed ADR citing
  DR-D19 that binds once you accept it (e.g. in the merge comment)."
  Before the question, the agent told the owner in chat that each build's DR-D19 targets "should come
  from its own seasons only" and that, with a stored production track, "the second canary (pre-window
  data reaches neither prior) can't pass". The descriptions of the other options, as offered:
  - "Talent, vague seed": "Keeps DR-D19's words. Each build re-runs the filter over the target season
    from a declared vague seed that uses no data, and the target is end-of-season talent with a
    known-noise correction. One sentence is added to Component 8 to allow the vague seed."
  - "Talent, population seed": "Keeps DR-D19's words exactly. The re-run starts from the build's own
    population prior, and the target is plain least squares on end-of-season talent. Because that seed
    is estimated on the same window, this needs a scoped exemption from the no-look-ahead rule that
    implements ratified DR-C10."
  - "Keep open": "The seed and estimand stay open, and DR-D19's text is unchanged. Recorded now: each
    build computes its targets only from its own window records, never from a stored production track.
    The feeder base can't be built in engine form until you decide, so P1-07's NCAA/rookie prior
    assembly and the feeder path of P1-12 are gated on it."

  Every option therefore computed each build's targets from that build's own window, and "Keep open"
  said so explicitly.
- **Answer 11 (transcription of answers 9 and 10; DR-D32 canaries).** Answer 8's direction came
  before answers 9 and 10, so the agent asked for it again. Answer 11 directs their transcription under
  the same rule-1 exception. It also confirms a reading that review added: both DR-D32 canaries hold
  the model version's learned constants fixed (κ_c, λ_c and the filter's `SSParams`). Those are
  calibrated by rolling origin, generally on seasons before the window, and are not window evidence.
  Apart from them, pre-window data reaches neither season-boundary prior.
